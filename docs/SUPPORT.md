# Free forever and optional support

Mnemion is **100% free, always**, for individuals, teams and organizations.
All product features, integrations and updates are included. There are no paid
tiers, per-seat charges, premium model selectors or subscriptions to unlock the
software. The source remains available under the [MIT license](../LICENSE).

Optional support does not purchase access, faster updates or a different edition.
The product page will offer one-time PayPal support starting at **€5**, with higher
amounts available. Downloading and using Mnemion never requires a contribution.
Only use the receiving link published on the official product page; do not send
money to an address found in an issue or an unofficial download.

PayPal handles the payment. Mnemion does not collect payment credentials, store
card or bank details, or enroll supporters in recurring payments. PayPal may
apply transaction or currency-conversion fees. PayPal payments reach the
recipient's PayPal account; bank withdrawals follow that account's own settings.
The product page enforces a €5 minimum in its form; a PayPal payment link is not
a guarantee that the payer cannot change the amount on PayPal itself.

The software's free policy is separate from infrastructure selected by its user.
Your own hosting, storage, cloud models or other third-party services may charge
you. Local models and self-hosted operation remain available. No paid model or
memory subscription is required by Mnemion.

The [roadmap](ROADMAP.md), [release notes](releases/4.0.0.md) and
[maintenance policy](MAINTENANCE.md) describe delivery and update status.
Feedback, documentation, reproducible bug reports and code contributions are
also useful ways to support the project. See [contributing](../CONTRIBUTING.md).
