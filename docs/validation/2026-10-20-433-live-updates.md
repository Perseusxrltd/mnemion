## The lab did not pass

The independently scheduled Windows lab started at 07:38 while the app was 4.33
and the service was still 4.32. It exhausted its 120-request bound while waiting
for indexing. The requests succeeded, but the three synthetic records had not
finished indexing, and cleanup shared the exhausted request allowance. The
acceptance driver retained this failure and stopped instead of forcing a new
cycle or writing a success receipt.

The worker was running and completing inference. Read-only database observations
subsequently found **234 pending embedding jobs and eight pending purges** behind
an existing FIFO queue; the active job was about 15 KB. Other spaces' newer
writes and physical purges were waiting behind that inventory. Counts are a
dated observation, not a capacity benchmark or a claim that the update lost data.

The private lab now waits for matching app/server versions, reserves 30 of its
existing 120 requests for cleanup, backs off indexing polls and journals accepted
withdrawal receipts. Cleanup verifies the recorded space, creating identity,
synthetic marker and run ID before withdrawing a record. The original failed
cycle and scripts were preserved. A cleanup-only operation is bounded separately;
it cannot turn the failed cycle into a passing one. At this checkpoint all three
withdrawals have been accepted; physical purge verification remains pending.

There were no production-memory writes or cloud-model calls in the lab. The
source [4.35 dispatch fix](2026-10-20-fair-indexing.md) addresses the backlog's
scheduling consequence. Full actual release/lab acceptance remains incomplete
until cleanup and a new bounded cycle are verified. This does not close ChatGPT
issue #18, restore the missing original office or establish fleet acceptance.
