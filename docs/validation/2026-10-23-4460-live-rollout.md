The [original Windows failure](2026-10-23-windows-host-client.md) and both
[rejected publication scans](2026-10-23-openssl-scan.md) remain preserved.
The later passing gates apply to their recorded inputs and do not replace
acceptance on the actual Windows/shared-host pair.

The ordinary Windows updater downloaded 4.46.0, and a guarded agent restart
applied it. The observer recorded the app at **4.46.0 at 05:53:35 UTC**.
This establishes the observed update and relaunch; the full paired checks
were still waiting for the host.

On the host, the candidate API and worker processes started at **06:12:56 UTC**
and entered supervisor `RUNNING` state at **06:12:58 UTC**. The API log records
successful Uvicorn application startup. The updater nevertheless ended the
attempt at its **60-second managed-readiness boundary** and restored the
previous runtime. Its history records **4.45.1 restored at 06:14:47 UTC**, with
`database_restored: false`. Supervisor `RUNNING` and API startup completion
alone did not establish the updater's complete readiness condition.

The **06:21 UTC** inspection found a heavily loaded one-CPU host and readiness
receipts for the restored 4.45.1 runtime. The inspected evidence shows no OOM
or spontaneous process crash. It does not establish the precise failed
readiness condition or prove that CPU load caused the rollback; diagnosis and
a managed-startup hotfix remain pending at this checkpoint.

The paired observer stopped for inspection at **06:17:03 UTC**, recording
Windows **4.46.0** and host **4.45.1**. Its dependent fresh-backup and adoption
jobs stopped at their prerequisites. They made **no controller adoption or
operating-system changes**. The updater's recovery record does not constitute
a fresh independently verified production backup.

Private publication, updater, readiness and observer receipts are retained;
raw host logs, identities and connection details are not included here.
Completion still requires the hotfix and its release gates, renewed actual
paired acceptance, a fresh independently verified backup, actual controller
deployment, and both custodians' stop/restart/start acceptance. All
[65 requirement texts and states](../PRODUCT_REQUIREMENTS.md) remain unchanged.
