an explicit trust set. Routine checks for an absent company policy also avoid
allocating filesystem exceptions. Every check still reads the current policy;
restrictive or invalid policies, other filesystem errors, user opt-out and TLS
verification retain their existing behavior.

problems before the changes. The corrected source passed two cold-process tests
under Node 22.22.0 and 95 focused Electron tests, with two platform skips. The
fixed source-app comparison completed both full memory workloads and the private
enabled report delivery, with cleanup verified. The recorded allocation samples
support these two corrections; they do not establish a measured RSS or CPU saving.

remain open. V08 stays **Building and inactive**; production collection,
destination and route remain false/null/null. The 2 MiB / 0.5% gates and all
