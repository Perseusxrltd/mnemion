# Mnemion 4.46.31

Browser telemetry now stops pending eligibility and sensor work when its owner
is cancelled. Opting out or leaving a page cannot start a new pressure observer
when an earlier eligibility check finishes. Existing privacy, connection,
resource and delivery limits continue to apply.

[Validation](../validation/2026-10-26-telemetry-browser-admission.md) reproduced
three cancellation failures before the fix. All 134 browser telemetry tests
and the frontend build passed. The actual compiled browser app passed ten
privacy and report-review checks across two launches and three memory-work
cycles. Cross-tab opt-out cleared the held report; one explicitly approved
four-byte report reached the private TLS collector unchanged; settings and
spent allowance survived restart. Fixture corrections and original failures
are retained with the evidence.

V08 remains **Building and inactive**. Production collection, certificate and
provider privacy, full resource-budget and installed-platform acceptance remain
open. All 66 requirements and existing limits are preserved. Consult
[release status](../RELEASE_STATUS.md) for published downloads.
