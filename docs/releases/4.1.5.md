# Mnemion 4.1.5

Desktop update checks recover sooner after a failed check or download. Previously,
a failed attempt could wait for the next normal six-hour check, even after the
network or release host recovered.

Supported desktop installations now make up to three additional attempts after
approximately one, five and fifteen minutes, with a small random delay to spread
downloads across an organization. Continued failures return to the regular
schedule. **Check for updates** starts a fresh attempt at any time.

A successful check or download clears pending retries. Pausing automatic updates
or quitting cancels them, and overlapping requests share the current check.
Signature verification, download limits, administrator policy, unsaved-work
protection and the normal safe-restart requirements remain in force.

See the [retry validation](../validation/2026-10-12-update-retries.md) and
[background/update guide](../service/updates.md). This change applies to desktop
update checks; managed servers and package-manager installations retain their
documented schedules. No database migration or model download is required.
