# Mnemion 4.46.50

Telemetry reports now withhold category estimates when their confidence
intervals cannot describe a valid distribution. Previously, each interval could
look plausible while the categories together implied a total above or below
100%. The publisher also rejects such invalid reports and leaves withheld
values unknown.

The [focused qualification](../validation/2026-10-29-telemetry-joint-consistency.md)
passed **78 checks**, covering the reproduced failures, privacy bounds, valid
historical reports and immutable publication. This detects impossible totals;
it does not authenticate senders or establish general resistance to poisoning.

Full telemetry acceptance remains open, with production collection disabled.
The existing installer-size limit and offline capabilities remain in place.
Source CI, native qualification and publication for this version are pending.
