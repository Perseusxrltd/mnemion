# Mnemion 4.46.41

The local telemetry inventory now includes transcript splitting and model
testing. `mnemion split` contributes one owned application attempt; dry runs and
`--no-telemetry` remain excluded. `mnemion llm test` reports success only for
the exact requested reply and otherwise records its bounded failure outcome.
Paths, transcripts, prompts and replies are not measurement fields.

The new catalog appends these categories after the original 94. Existing saved
cells retain their meanings and spent daily budgets; browser queues move to
catalog v2 on their next collection write. Four-byte wire v2 keeps its existing
coordinates, weights and domains. Closed report schema 4 includes the new
labels, while saved schema 1–3 reports remain readable without rewriting them.

The source inventory now maps all 96 categories and accounts for 701 actions:
398 mapped, 295 excluded, eight delegated and none unmeasured. These counts
describe reviewed source boundaries. Production product collection remains
**disabled** while resource, privacy, deployment, runtime and platform
qualification continues.

The [compatibility report](../validation/2026-10-27-telemetry-catalog-compatibility.md)
records 322 passing focused checks and the remaining limits. The
[controls guide](../guides/product-telemetry-controls.md) explains the privacy
choices; the [roadmap](../ROADMAP.md) and automatically maintained
[release status](../RELEASE_STATUS.md) show outstanding work and published builds.
